## Brand & Style

This design system embodies mindful tranquility, spiritual grounding, and intentional living tailored for a modern Muslim audience. It blends contemporary Scandinavian minimalism with organic Islamic motifs—prioritizing breathability, contemplative pacing, and spiritual clarity over digital noise.

### Brand Attributes
- **Contemplative & Serene:** Uses calm greens, gentle pastel accents, soft ambient glows, and spacious layouts that foster deep reflection and focus (*Niyyah*).
- **Graceful & Purposeful:** Every interaction feels deliberate, unhurried, and supportive without gamification anxiety or aggressive prompts.
- **Warm & Organic:** Pairs crisp white containers against soft mint washes, enhanced by nature-inspired scenic banners and rounded, welcoming forms.

### Design Movement
The visual language merges **Warm Minimalism** with subtle **Soft Tactility**. It relies on generous border radii (24px to 32px), subtle tinted surfaces, and featherlight borders rather than dense drop shadows, keeping cards floating effortlessly across the subtle mint-tinted backdrop.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from the Islamic tradition of verdant serenity, featuring deep forest evergreen accents balanced by uplifting, soothing botanical undertones and soft pastel categories.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary & Deep Accent (`#14532d`, `#1b4d3e`):** Grounded evergreen shades reserved for key CTAs, bottom navigation active indicators, primary selection states, and emphasized headers.
- **Canvas & Surface Washes:** 
  - Canvas Body: `#f4fbf7` (a luminous, calming mint-tinted off-white).
  - Elevated Container / Cards: `#ffffff` (crisp white providing clean contrast).
  - Subtle Highlights / Active Tints: `#e8f5e9`, `#dcfce7`, `#f0fdf4`.
  - Subtle Borders & Separators: `#e2eee6` and `#d3e7dc`.
- **Text Hierarchy:**
  - High Emphasis: Deep pine-slate `#0f291e`.
  - Medium / Muted Body: Slate eucalyptus `#4a6b5d`.
  - Light Labels & Placeholders: `#7d9b8e`.
- **Category Semantic Pastels:**
  - Sage Mint: Surface `#e8f5e9`, Icon `#1b4d3e` (Character & Manners / Patience)
  - Warm Amber: Surface `#fef3c7`, Icon `#d97706` (Self-Control / Mindfulness)
  - Soft Rose: Surface `#fee2e2`, Icon `#e11d48` (Family & Relationships / Hearts)
  - Soft Lavender: Surface `#f3e8ff`, Icon `#7c3aed` (Self-Reflection / Remembrance)
  - Sky Blue: Surface `#e0f2fe`, Icon `#0284c7` (Health & Wellbeing)

## Typography

Typography prioritizes open letterforms, human warmth, and exceptional legibility across bilingual contexts. Plus Jakarta Sans provides crisp modern geometry with soft, welcoming terminals that align with the platform's spiritual and tranquil character.

### Application Rules
- **Display & Headlines:** Used for serene screen headers, intention statements, and spiritual quotations. Set in deep evergreen tones (`#0f291e`) with tight letter-tracking (`-0.01em` to `-0.02em`).
- **Body & Contemplation:** Set with generous line heights (`1.5` to `1.6`) to prevent visual fatigue and support relaxed reading of scripture, reflections, and context descriptions.
- **Interactive Labels & Badges:** Rendered in semi-bold weights with proportional scaling for clarity at small badge sizes (11px–12px).

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built around mobile-first utility, using an 8pt rhythmic grid with a base margin of 16px to 20px on mobile viewports.

### Architectural Rhythm
- **Canvas Structure:** Fixed max-width on mobile (390px–428px viewport standard) centered on larger devices with fluid gutter constraints.
- **Card Flow:** Vertical stack gaps default to `space-md` (16px) or `space-sm` (12px) between sequential intention items, maintaining clear individual tap targets.
- **Section Pacing:** Group separations employ `space-xl` (24px) to provide natural breathability between category blocks, banners, and supporting resources.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses calm tonal layering and soft, daylight-inspired ambient shadows rather than stark elevation tiers.

### Tiers & Techniques
- **Base Canvas:** Flat `#f4fbf7` providing the serene colored foundation.
- **Floating Cards (Default):** Crisp `#ffffff` container bounded by a delicate 1px border (`#e3ede7`) paired with an ultra-soft botanical shadow: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(20, 83, 45, 0.04), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(20, 83, 45, 0.02)`.
- **Selected Card State:** Elevated with an emerald wash outline (1.5px solid `#14532d` or `#2e7d32`), filled with a faint green sheen (`#f0fdf4`), removing shadow to feel intentionally anchored.
- **Persistent Bottom Bar & Floating CTAs:** Elevated through gentle translucent backdrop blur (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.88)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`) and a feathered upward ambient wash (`0 -8px 24px rgba(20, 83, 45, 0.04)`).
- **Modals & Drawers:** High surface white with smooth 32px top-radii, elevated over an organic dimmed barrier (`rgba(15, 41, 30, 0.35)`).

## Shapes

The shape system relies on generous, humanized curvature that softens digital rigidity into peaceful organic surfaces.

### Shape Scale
- **Interactive Pills / Badges / Toggle Switches:** Full pill curve (`rounded-full`, 9999px).
- **Cards, Intention Items & Hero Banners:** `rounded-2xl` (16px–20px) to `rounded-3xl` (24px), producing inviting, tactile surfaces.
- **Modal Sheets & Overlays:** `rounded-t-[28px]` or `rounded-t-[32px]`.
- **Icon Enclosures:** Nested circular or squircle containers (40px–48px size with 50% or 16px radius) centered within list cells.

## Components

### Segmented Segment Controllers (Intentions vs Content)
- Capsule enclosure with soft background `#e8f5e9` and 8px interior padding.
- Active toggle button shifts to pure `#ffffff` with subtle elevation shadow, forest green text (`#14532d`), and matching icon.
- Inactive segment maintains muted green text (`#4a6b5d`) without background fill.

### Intention List Items
- **Structure:** Crisp white background, rounded-2xl geometry, horizontal padding 16px, vertical padding 14px.
- **Left Slot:** Custom rounded-lg checkbox paired with a 44px tinted circular badge housing thematic line-art icons (mosque, hands in dua, heart, seedling, mountain).
- **Center Content:** Two-line hierarchy with primary intention title in semi-bold slate and secondary metadata/tagging in muted tone.
- **Right Trailing:** Subtle quick-action icons (favorite heart, share outline, chevron) in soft slate `#7d9b8e`.
- **Multi-select State:** Entire card background subtly shifts to `#f0fdf4` with 1.5px border `#14532d`.

### Hero & Thematic Scenic Banners
- Landscape ratio (typically 16:9 or 2.5:1 aspect) displaying serene watercolor and photographic panoramas (morning mist over hills, dawn skies, tranquil lakes, wildflower meadows).
- Rounded with 20px–24px corners and subtle inset gradient overlays ensuring text contrast when typography overlays the image.

### Buttons & Interactive CTAs
- **Primary Action:** Solid forest emerald `#14532d`, pure white text, pill-shaped (rounded-full) or rounded-2xl, 52px height for thumb ergonomics, subtle active press down-scale (0.98).
- **Secondary / Ghost Action:** Soft mint wash `#e8f5e9` with `#14532d` text, or transparent with hairline green border `#d3e7dc`.
- **Pill Chips / Media Filter Tabs:** Rounded-full chips (All, Text, Video, Audio) displaying count badges; selected state is deep forest `#1b4d3e` with white text, inactive is pure white with light mint stroke.

### Reminder Scheduling & Action Cross-Sells
- Modular selection cards detailing timing frequencies (Daily, Weekly, Custom, No reminder).
- Expandable nested accordion lists for "Suggested Actions" attached to each selected intention, complete with estimated commitment durations ("4 min", "2 min") to maintain approachable micro-habits.
